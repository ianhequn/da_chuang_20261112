 Title: MAESTRO: Orchestrating Robotics Modules with Coding Agents for Zero-Shot Generalist Robots 
 Authors: Junyao Shi , Rujia Yang , Kaitian Chao , Selina Bingqing Wan , Yifei Shao , Jiahui Lei , Jianing Qian , Long Le , Pratik Chaudhari , Kostas Daniilidis , Chuan Wen , Dinesh Jayaraman View a PDF of the paper titled MAESTRO: Orchestrating Robotics Modules with Coding Agents for Zero-Shot Generalist Robots, by Junyao Shi and 11 other authors 
 View PDF 
 HTML (experimental) 
 Abstract: Today's dominant route to generalist robots scales robotics data to train end-to-end policies. We instead use a vision-language model (VLM) as a coding agent that writes robot programs using a curated library of perception, planning, and control tools. MAESTRO's orchestration harness packages multi-step perception and action procedures into callable tools and guides their use through a plan-act-replan loop, using visual and execution feedback to verify outcomes and recover from failures. With this tool library and harness, MAESTRO matches or surpasses state-of-the-art vision-language-action (VLA) models zero-shot in our tabletop comparison and performs diverse mobile manipulation tasks, without collecting new teleoperation data or invoking a VLA tool. Ablations show that expanding the tool library improves average task progress with the full harness, while adding tools without it does not reliably improve progress. Case studies illustrate MAESTRO's potential for autonomous code improvement across trials, faster execution with VLA tools, and automatic robot data generation. We show that expanding robotics tool libraries together with their orchestration harnesses offers a complementary route to general-purpose robots alongside scaling end-to-end robot training data.
